A slower kind of internet

Some things deserve more than an open tab. An essay you want to return to. A conversation that changes your mind. A small discovery worth keeping.

This is a place for those things. Save a page, settle into the words, and let a voice carry you through. The original page and a quiet reading view belong together.

The conversation belongs here, too

A page does not always end with the article. Sometimes the most useful part is a question below it, or a reply from someone who sees the world differently.

Links carry meaning as well. Visit this example link to see how we keep its destination attached to its words.
